Art in Progress

A social platform for young artists — targeted networking, curated opportunities, and structured feedback.

Check Out Our Landing Page: artinprogress.media

Abstract

Young artists have talent and passion. Yet, they lack exposure, and turning their craft into a career can feel like navigating a maze.

Opportunities are scattered all over different platforms. Connections can be hard to find. Feedback often stops at likes and comments. To succeed, an artist may have the talent. They may have the skill. They may have the drive. But without the right network, resources, and guidance, knowing how to take the next step can be difficult.

Art in Progress was created to address this gap.

Art in Progress is a social platform designed specifically for young artists, built around three things they need to make real progress: targeted networking, curated opportunities, and structured feedback.

Rather than relying on algorithms designed for general social media, Art in Progress helps artists find people who are relevant to their creative journey, including potential mentors, collaborators, and peers. The platform also brings the opportunities artists need into one curated space, including challenges, competitions, career boards, personalized dashboards, publications, discussion threads, and so much more.

By bringing connection, opportunity, and community together, Art in Progress reduces the time artists spend searching for ways to advance and gives them more time to focus on their creative work.

We conducted more than 30 interviews with artists to understand their challenges, needs, and experiences, then launched an exclusive early version with 20 diverse artists to gather feedback and refine our approach.

We have since developed and launched our landing page, and are continuing to build our MVP, create our marketing materials, and connect with artists in our local community. Each step has helped us turn what started as an idea into something extraordinary.

Our goal is not to create another place for artists to post their work. It is to create a place where artists can build the relationships, find the opportunities, and receive the feedback that can turn their creative journey into a career.

Art in Progress is still a work in progress. And that’s exactly the point. We are building it alongside the artists it is meant to serve, with the goal of giving young creatives a clearer path from potential to progress.

An artist’s journey should not end at potential. It should begin there. Art in Progress exists so they don't have to take the next step alone.
