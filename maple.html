M.A.P.L.E.

My Advanced Personal Logic Engine — A personal AI assistant built to turn ideas, information, and everyday tasks into action.

About the Project

Have you seen Iron Man? Then you probably know J.A.R.V.I.S.—a highly intelligent system designed to understand its user, process information, and help turn ideas into action. M.A.P.L.E. started with a similar question: what if I could build my own?

I’m constantly thinking about project ideas, research questions, problems I want to solve, and goals I want to accomplish. I wanted a system that could help me find answers quickly, organize what I learn, optimize my time, and ultimately help me execute—not just recommend what I should do.

So, I started building M.A.P.L.E.

Unlike a traditional AI assistant that primarily answers questions, manages a calendar, or sends reminders, M.A.P.L.E. is designed to become a data-driven, decision-making system. The long-term goal is for it to learn from my patterns, understand context, make informed predictions, and help me make better decisions based on what I actually do—not just what I tell it to do.

What It Does

M.A.P.L.E. is designed around a few core capabilities:

  • Natural-language interaction — communicate with the assistant conversationally rather than through rigid commands.
  • Context & memory — retain useful information so conversations and tasks can build on previous interactions.
  • Tool use — connect AI reasoning with software tools so the assistant can do more than generate text.
  • Task execution — move from suggesting what I should do toward actually helping complete tasks.
  • Personalization — adapt to my projects, workflows, and preferences instead of behaving like a generic assistant.
  • Extensibility — build the system as a modular foundation that can grow as I experiment with new AI capabilities.
